Privacy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Quiet Pages collects no personal data. It has no analytics, ads, or tracking, and it makes no network requests.

Entries are stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB), encrypted with a key that stays in memory while the journal is unlocked. The passphrase is never saved.

We can't see, recover, or delete your entries. There is no account and no server copy. If you forget the passphrase, nobody can open the journal for you.

Backups

Backups are files you control. A backup stays encrypted. Reading it takes the passphrase that was in use when that file was exported. Quiet Pages does not receive or store those files.

Children

Quiet Pages is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Where the app is hosted

If you open Quiet Pages from a website (for example itch.io, or the web host of the private web version), that host's own privacy policy covers its website and server logs. Quiet Pages itself sends nothing about you or your entries.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. The previous text is replaced on this page.
